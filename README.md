## 🧠 About Me

<img align="right" width="190" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dnbdcic4r/image/upload/v1778166139/my-photo.jpg" alt="Md. Asaduzzaman Shuvo" style="border-radius:50%"/>

I'm an **AI/ML researcher and engineer** with a **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering** from **United International University (UIU)**, Dhaka.

My research sits at the intersection of **NLP and Vision-Language Models**, with a focus on **low-resource languages** and **real-world multimodal understanding**:

- 📚 &nbsp;Building **datasets & benchmarks** that reveal where and why LLMs fail — Bangla text generation, linguistic register, and culturally grounded reasoning
- ⚙️ &nbsp;Making compact models more capable with **LoRA / QLoRA, quantization, and reasoning distillation**
- 🌍 &nbsp;Applying **explainable VLMs** to environmental & public-health problems — mosquito breeding-site analysis and floating-waste detection
- 💼 &nbsp;Currently: **AI/ML Engineer Intern @ Parallax Labs**

> 🔎 **Open to:** Research collaborations · Internships · Research Assistant (RA) positions · AI/ML Engineer roles
