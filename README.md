## 🧠 About Me

<img align="right" width="200" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dnbdcic4r/image/upload/w_440,h_440,c_fill,g_face,r_max,bo_8px_solid_rgb:60a5fa,f_png/v1778166139/my-photo.jpg" alt="Md. Asaduzzaman Shuvo"/>

I'm an **AI/ML researcher and engineer** with a **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering** from **United International University (UIU)**, Dhaka.

My research sits where **NLP meets Vision-Language Models**, with a focus on **low-resource languages** and **real-world multimodal understanding**.

- 📚 &nbsp;I build **datasets & benchmarks** that reveal *where and why* LLMs fail — Bangla generation, linguistic register, culturally grounded reasoning
- ⚙️ &nbsp;I make compact models stronger with **LoRA / QLoRA, quantization & reasoning distillation**
- 🌍 &nbsp;I apply **explainable VLMs** to public-health & environmental problems — mosquito breeding sites, floating waste
- 💼 &nbsp;Now: **AI/ML Engineer Intern @ Parallax Labs**
